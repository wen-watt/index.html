PoW's Finality
Proof of Work (PoW): Finality is achieved when a block is added to the blockchain and sufficiently confirmed by subsequent blocks (e.g., 6 confirmations in Bitcoin). It's probabilistic, with security increasing as more blocks are mined, but it's slow (minutes to hours) and energy-intensive due to computational competition.
