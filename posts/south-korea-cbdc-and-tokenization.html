South Korea: CBDC and tokenization

Confirmed:

  • South Korea's National Assembly passed amendments in January 2026 that established a legal framework for security token offerings, clearing a path for blockchain-based issuance and trading of tokenized securities in the country. Those amendments were promulgated the following month and are scheduled to take effect in February 2027.
  • South Korea is also planning a 2027 pilot to connect tokenized government bonds with the Bank of Korea's wholesale CBDC platform. The plan was included in the Ministry of Economy and Finance's 2026 Second Half Economic Growth Strategy.
  • Shinhan Asset Management signed a four-party memorandum of understanding with the Solana Foundation, Etherfuse and Orca on Aug. 21 to test a Korean won-denominated tokenized fund modeled on BlackRock's BUIDL.

Correction to the original text:

  • This is a proof-of-concept, not a launched product — both MOUs are non-binding and limited to offshore technical validation.
  • The agreement comes seven days after Shinhan signed a nearly identical deal with Plume, a rival tokenization-focused blockchain, for the same underlying fund. So Solana is being tested in parallel, not exclusively.
  • The state CBDC pilot and Shinhan's commercial pilot are two separate, formally unconnected tracks.

Token-2022 as the technical foundation

Confirmed:

  • Solana's Token-2022 program allows issuers to embed compliance controls directly into a token's onchain logic using "extensions." The critical extension is the transfer hook — a programmable rule that runs on every token transfer, checking whether the receiving wallet is on an approved allowlist. If the wallet hasn't completed KYC verification, the transfer is rejected by the protocol itself. Combined with the default account state extension — which starts every new token account in a "frozen" state until the issuer approves it — this creates a permission architecture on a permissionless public blockchain. Orca's permissioned Whirlpool pools add a second layer: only wallets holding a verified Civic Pass can provide liquidity or trade.
  • On March 17, 2026, the SEC issued a joint interpretation with the CFTC establishing a token taxonomy of five categories, explicitly identifying Solana as a "digital commodity" that is not itself a security.

Conclusion

All three links are confirmed by independent sources, but the original text overstated how far the Korean events had progressed. The technical chain holds: Token-2022 extensions, first battle-tested on WEN in 2024, became the basis for Shinhan's institutional compliance pilot in 2026 — which runs parallel to, not inside, the Bank of Korea's 2027 wholesale CBDC plan. 기술적 기반으로서의 Token-2022

확인된 사실:

  • Solana의 Token-2022 프로그램은 발행자가 "확장 기능(extensions)"을 통해 컴플라이언스 통제를 토큰의 온체인 로직에 직접 내장할 수 있게 합니다. 핵심 확장 기능은 transfer hook로, 모든 토큰 전송 시 실행되어 수신 지갑이 승인된 allowlist에 있는지 확인합니다. 지갑이 KYC 인증을 완료하지 않았다면 전송은 프로토콜 자체에 의해 거부됩니다. 여기에 default account state 확장 기능(모든 신규 토큰 계정을 발행자가 승인할 때까지 "동결" 상태로 시작)이 결합되어, 허가 없는 퍼블릭 블록체인 위에서도 권한 관리 구조가 작동합니다. Orca의 허가형 Whirlpool 풀은 두 번째 계층을 추가하여, 검증된 Civic Pass를 보유한 지갑만 유동성을 제공하거나 거래할 수 있습니다.
  • 2026년 3월 17일 SEC는 CFTC와 공동으로 5개 범주의 토큰 분류 체계를 확립하는 해석을 발표했으며, Solana를 그 자체로는 증권이 아닌 "디지털 상품(digital commodity)"으로 명시적으로 분류했습니다.
