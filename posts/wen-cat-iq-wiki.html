Mapping the money graph, openly.

IQ.Wiki introduces Ecosystem Map — an interactive, non-commercial graph that lays every verified relationship around a wiki in plain sight. First stop: the Wen network.

Most crypto research still lives in disconnected fragments — a founder mentioned in one article, an exchange listing buried in another, a chain dependency nobody bothers to diagram. IQ.Wiki, the community-run, non-commercial encyclopedia for Web3, has closed that gap with Ecosystem Map: a live, interactive graph attached to every wiki, now live on the Wen page.

Ecosystem Map — Wen's 16 verified connections, grouped by category

01 A Map, Not a Marketing Funnel

The design choice that matters most is what Ecosystem Map doesn't do: it doesn't rank sponsors, sell placement, or push users toward any exchange or token. As a non-commercial public resource, IQ.Wiki has no incentive to bias the graph — every node exists because it is documented, not because it paid to be there. That distinction is what turns a visualization into a research instrument.

02 Centralized Markets, Decentralized Rails — One View

Wen's graph makes a point that used to take five open tabs to verify: a decentralized memecoin, a decentralized host chain, and a centralized-adjacent exchange all sit on the same map, with the exact relationship labeled between them.

03 Why Openness Builds Trust

In a market where due diligence is often a chore, a free, sourced, always-on relationship map is a quiet but significant upgrade to public trust infrastructure. Nothing is paywalled. Nothing requires an account. The logic is simple: verifiability is the product.

The takeaway: Ecosystem Map doesn't tell users what to think about a project — it shows them exactly how the pieces connect, and lets them decide.

04 What This Means for the Market's Future

As on-chain activity multiplies across chains, exchanges, and founders, the ability to see relationships at a glance — rather than reconstruct them from scattered sources — becomes core research infrastructure, not a nice-to-have. By rolling Ecosystem Map out first on Wen and extending it wiki by wiki, IQ.Wiki is building toward a fully cross-referenced, community-verified map of the entire crypto industry: useful to today's researchers, and foundational for whoever builds the next generation of Web3 tools.
